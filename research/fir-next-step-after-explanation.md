## What is sufficient, and what the explanation changes

The central question has an answer: under this fixed exact-output contract, giving direct accumulation two certified native-width partial sums changes the apparent direct/transposed choice. Corrected direct wins both GCC10 configurations; demand Hcub wins both GCC15 configurations. Relative to corrected direct, Hcub's modeled-energy cost is +4.33%/+5.00% with GCC10 and −24.06%/−5.23% with GCC15, for blocks 1/64. These four admitted configurations support a local comparison rather than a general selection rule. [Claims and comparison boundaries](fir-sharing-probe/explanation-check/claims.md).

The explanation now supplies more than a compiler-dependent win table. For the two block1 direct binaries, established call/path frequencies and stack-slot value flows show that GCC15 retains and rotates history samples through stack slots. Extra executed direct-SP transfers explain all +13,878 RAM reads and +19,275 RAM writes, and 85.0151% of the modeled-energy increase. The residual is explicitly +10,667 instructions, +131 taken branches and +10,559.790252 modeled nJ. The compiler pass and reason for stack placement remain unproved. This is additive cost attribution, not the predicted benefit of deleting the accesses. [Final explanation decision](fir-sharing-probe/explanation-check/final-decision.md).

**My novelty judgment:** the explanation materially improves credibility and completeness, while leaving field-level novelty modest. It connects the corrected ranking to emitted sample lifetimes and executed traffic; it does not transform the project into a new method. There is now a defensible narrative: initial apparent advantage → stronger arithmetic comparator → mixed result → bounded generated-code explanation → limitations. That is more informative than reporting that two compiler versions choose different winners. The earlier assessment explicitly recommended stopping after this bounded explanation even if it found ordinary extra stack traffic; that condition has now been met. [Earlier decision](fir-post-join-research-assessment.md), [completed claims](fir-sharing-probe/explanation-check/claims.md).

## What would justify more work or a pivot

**Bounded missing analysis:** warranted if drafting exposes a numerical inconsistency, inaccessible evidence needed for reproduction, or an assertion that lacks its promised source. First resolve it from saved artifacts or narrow the assertion. The unresolved residual and compiler pass do not block the accepted partial explanation; omit stronger causal claims instead of making their proof a new prerequisite. [Accepted stopping boundary](fir-sharing-probe/explanation-check/final-decision.md).

**New experiments:** warranted only for a newly chosen claim that existing evidence cannot answer. A claim about generalization across filters/workloads would require a separately specified evaluation with equally strong controls; a claim that a particular pass causes the traffic would require suitable compiler diagnostics/counterfactual evidence. Those would extend the question. They are unnecessary for the present bounded manuscript and should not be run merely to increase row count or seek a larger saving. [Existing scope and limits](fir-sharing-probe/explanation-check/claims.md), [prior assessment](fir-post-join-research-assessment.md).

**Research pivot:** justified if the actual evaluation requirements explicitly demand a new algorithm or a broader demonstrated contribution, or if the authors choose that different objective. Before investing in it, identify a concrete unresolved problem against closest prior work and state what would falsify the proposed solution. Under the currently stated college-project scope, my judgment is that a pivot has lower value than completing this credible, modest case study. No presumed publication standard, grade threshold or undisclosed professor requirement enters that recommendation.

This note changes no implementation or result. Numerical evidence and recorded verification outcomes are inherited from the linked completed checks, not independently rerun here. The fresh contribution is the focused source recheck and the next-deliverable judgment.
