# Post-join research assessment

20 October 2026. Research judgment after the completed arithmetic-fairness join. Scope: FIR only, simulator-only college empirical paper, publication optional, professor as final evaluator rather than project guide. This note changes no implementation or result.

## Decision

**Go for a carefully bounded college empirical paper; no-go for presenting the current work as a new FIR algorithm or an established substantial field contribution.** The correction makes the work more credible and the headline less impressive. It establishes an original, reproducible observation about this artifact. Whether that observation is sufficiently valuable for the assignment depends on the evaluator's criteria; the literature does not decide the grade.

My confidence is high in that distinction and moderate in the project's eventual paper value. A coherent case study is already possible. A strong publication novelty claim remains weak, and unlimited time does not change the overlap with established techniques. I would finish the explanatory paper rather than restart the project merely because the result is mixed. If the assignment explicitly requires a new algorithm, the current direction does not meet that requirement.

The strongest defensible claim is:

> In one bit-exact, full-int16 48-tap FIR case on a pinned Cortex-M0 model, specializing direct accumulation into certified native-width partial sums changes the implementation ranking and substantially reduces an apparent transposed advantage. After that correction, direct versus transposed sharing has compiler-dependent instruction, memory and modeled-energy costs: partial direct wins both GCC 10 configurations, while demand Hcub wins both GCC 15 configurations. Therefore, in this case neither a wide final result nor a smaller constant-multiplication graph is sufficient to justify the implementation choice.

This is a **case-study contribution**, not a universal selection rule. Its useful content is the demonstrated failure of an inference under a stronger control, followed by a reproducible account of the corrected tradeoff. The broad lesson that arithmetic counts and representations interact with generated-code costs is already established; the exact combined evidence here is the original empirical work. [Completed join](fir-sharing-probe/independent-check/join-review.md), [verified comparison](fir-sharing-probe/independent-check/comparison.md), [prior positioning](fir-sharing-probe/independent-check/positioning.md).

## What the completed result adds

All four performance rows were independently admitted; arithmetic and emitted code were reviewed. For the frozen groups `[0,2)` and `[2,48)`, bounds 418,709,504 and 1,957,265,408 admit int32 partials, while the full absolute bound 2,375,974,912 does not fit int32. Widening both partials before the final addition preserves the specified single quantizer. This is a valid stronger comparator, not permission to overflow intermediate C expressions. [Arithmetic and evidence review](fir-sharing-probe/independent-check/join-review.md).

| Compiler | Block | Hcub modeled cost relative to corrected direct | Tested winner |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| GCC 10 | 1 | +4.33% | Partial direct |
| GCC 10 | 64 | +5.00% | Partial direct |
| GCC 15 | 1 | −24.06% | Demand Hcub |
| GCC 15 | 64 | −5.23% | Demand Hcub |

The old GCC15/block64 32.94% library-relative saving becomes 5.23% against corrected direct. The old percentage remains valid with its named CMSIS comparator; it cannot be attributed wholly to Hcub. Ordinary transposition beats corrected direct only at GCC15/block1. The original bank's 12/24 result has not become a bank-wide comparison against equally specialized direct controls. [Verified comparison](fir-sharing-probe/independent-check/comparison.md), [join limitations](fir-sharing-probe/independent-check/join-review.md).

The resource result is stronger than a win count. At GCC10/block64, narrowing direct lowers modeled cost from 990.40 to 575.81 nJ/output and RAM writes from 25,497 to 1,848. Hcub performs fewer instructions and MULs than corrected direct but more RAM traffic and loses under the model. Conversely, the identical partial-direct source has 198,670 instructions, 29,304 reads and 17,225 writes at GCC10/block1, versus 242,490/43,182/36,500 with GCC15; the frame grows from 72 to 240 bytes. These are concrete leads for explanation, not proof of a compiler defect or a complete attribution to register spills. [Reviewed resource evidence](fir-sharing-probe/independent-check/join-review.md).

If compiler version is itself selectable, a different decision question arises: GCC15 Hcub versus GCC10 partial direct is approximately 3.97% lower modeled energy at block1 and 2.30% at block64, calculated from the same verified table. The 24.06% fixed-GCC15 advantage is real within that comparison, but much of its prominence depends on GCC15's more expensive direct code. Keep these questions separate. Neither comparison supplies physical measurement uncertainty. [Underlying rows](fir-sharing-probe/independent-check/comparison.md).

## Are compiler-dependent winners substantive?

**Substantive as a result that changes the local implementation decision; routine as a general research mechanism.** The 24.06% GCC15/block1 gap is not merely rounding, and the GCC10 reversal prevents a misleading universal transposed story. But “compiler versions produce different spills/code” alone would be a thin paper. Prior FIR work already demonstrates different performance for equivalent arithmetic through locality and in-register organization, and Hcub already acknowledges register pressure as an unoptimized objective. [SPIRAL FIR §§4–5](https://spiral.ece.cmu.edu/pub-spiral/pubfile/fir.pdf), [Hcub §1.2](https://spiral.ece.cmu.edu/pubs/synth.pdf).

My inference is that the contribution becomes useful when it answers **which part of the apparent benefit survives equal arithmetic treatment, and where the remaining compiled cost comes from**, with arithmetic proof, same-graph controls, exact output checks and unfavorable rows available for inspection. It remains weak if it stops at “GCC10 likes one form and GCC15 another.” A new target, more filters or a larger table would not by themselves fix that weakness.

The exactness checks justify admissibility for the saved artifacts together with the arithmetic reasoning; they do not turn the 257-sample stream into a representative steady-state workload. Conclusions concern the stated zero-history finite stream and model, not all full-int16 signals or physical device energy. [Protocol and limits](fir-sharing-probe/independent-check/join-review.md).

## Single most useful bounded next step

**Complete one explanatory audit of the existing GCC10 versus GCC15 block1 partial-direct binaries.** Freeze the filter, C source, flags, stream and all four rankings. Use the saved disassemblies/events to account for the difference in dynamic instructions and RAM traffic by code region and access purpose: retained FIR state/input, stack-resident temporaries or spills, arithmetic and quantization/control. Trace representative stack slots back to values rather than inferring spill causation from frame size. Reconcile that accounting to the existing total counters and show a short annotated pair of code excerpts. This is an explanation task, not another benchmark campaign or an optimizer.

Why this pair: it tests whether the strongest headline is mainly a direct-code compiler deterioration, or a more informative representation/width interaction. Existing aggregate evidence makes both plausible but does not separate them completely. The deliverable should be one compact figure/table and an honest causal limit. If the saved evidence cannot distinguish a cause, state the unresolved cause; do not label correlation a compiler bug. [Saved evidence location and resource leads](fir-sharing-probe/independent-check/join-review.md).

Stop after that bounded explanation and write the college paper. If the explanation is only ordinary extra stack traffic, still report the corrected case study, but describe its original contribution as empirical characterization and baseline correction, with modest novelty. This is not a direct replication of a published experiment. If a substantial new algorithm is mandatory, change the research question explicitly rather than extending benchmarks until a favorable story appears. No advisor feedback, hardware purchase or publication promise is required for this next step.
