# Candidate A: literature check of empirical energy-bound reliability

Checked 19 October 2026. This is a targeted adversarial check, not a systematic review. Read with [the proposed question](./RESEARCH_QUESTION_BRAINSTORM.md) and [earlier prior-art notes](./RESEARCH_QUESTION_PRIOR_ART.md).

## Verdict and bounded question

**Reframe and conditionally retain as an independent replication and evaluation study; do not present the current idea as a new uncertainty method or an established research gap.** Direct WCEC work already addresses empirical safety, pessimism, training support and outliers. Adjacent runtime work now includes conformal upper bounds and distribution-shift experiments. General selective prediction already formalizes the rejection/usefulness trade-off. Finding no paper with exactly our dataset, split and rejection rule would be insufficient novelty evidence.

The defensible question is narrower:

> For the released MSP430FR5969 measurements, after reproducing the published feature construction and deduplication, does removing residual source/feature-template overlap change held-out energy-underestimation rates at matched bound inflation? If so, can a frozen code-only support policy improve the violation–inflation–retention frontier over calibrated global margins on independently held-out groups?

This tests a concrete deployment assumption: whether reliability on other blocks in a mixed corpus transfers to structurally different blocks. It does not ask whether ML can predict energy, whether new programs can be tested, or whether abstention can ever reduce error.

## What is already answered, and what is not

WORTEX's 80/20 block evaluation is supplemented by separate whole-program tests; “never evaluated on unseen programs” is false. Its extreme quantile gives zero observed block underestimation with WCEC MAPE around 97–99%; the lower quantile trades this for tighter predictions and some violations. Its explanation experiment also warns that extreme conservatism can favor block length over instruction effects. Adding test data to training in a later diagnostic is not a fresh held-out result. These observations motivate measuring an entire usefulness frontier; they do not establish that the published bounds fail under our proposed splits. [WORTEX §§5–6](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/01oasics/oasics-vol121-wcet2024/OASIcs.WCET.2024.1/OASIcs.WCET.2024.1.pdf)

The inspected direct work does not supply a validated, automatic “unknown instruction” safety contract. Equally, absence of such a rule in these texts is not evidence that the general idea is new. Separate genuinely unsupported instruction/address-mode features from unusual combinations of supported features. The former may reduce to ordinary vocabulary checking; the latter is the scientifically harder prediction problem.

Conformal methods do not erase the distinction. [Weighted conformal prediction under covariate shift](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2019/hash/8fb21ee7a2207526da55a679f0332de2-Abstract.html) already requires likelihood-ratio information and an appropriate shift model; [beyond-exchangeability theory](https://www.stat.cmu.edu/~ryantibs/papers/nexcp.pdf) quantifies coverage degradation. Neither supplies arbitrary-family-shift certification. Selecting accepted blocks also changes the population: report reliability among accepted cases explicitly, rather than infer it from overall marginal calibration.

## Feasible evidence and required baselines

The [author-deposited artifact](https://zenodo.org/records/11066623), published 25 April 2024, provides energy/time CSV labels and instruction JSON. A local read of the existing download independently confirms 30,085 rows, 9,390 measured ID prefixes, `nb_samples=40` throughout, and `energy_per_inst` matching `max_energy/nb_inst` within rounding throughout. The paper describes 100 measurements. These are replication/version questions, not grounds to declare the paper invalid. Use named raw columns and document the discrepancy before claiming faithful reproduction.

First reproduce published features, quantile objectives and deduplication. Audit both canonical instruction sequences and feature-vector collisions: WORTEX's instruction-proportion representation can make different sequences indistinguishable. Establish whether measured source prefixes really support useful groups; they are identifiers, not proof of independent projects. Freeze all grouping and support rules without test energy labels. Compare random-block, source-separated and source-plus-template-separated splits, and report the induced distribution shifts, especially instruction count and rare modes.

Minimum baselines are published QLR/GB/MLP, instruction-count bounds, a constant worst observed energy-per-instruction envelope fitted only on training data, calibrated additive/multiplicative global margins, and elementary support-distance or unseen-feature rejection. A conformal residual wrapper is an established baseline. Compare thresholds chosen on independent calibration groups, and report underestimation frequency **and severity**, overestimation quantiles, retained fraction, and fallback outcomes with source/cluster uncertainty. Measured-time features have a different information budget and require separate labeling.

Rejection alone produces no energy bound. A fallback must state its operational consequence: require measurement/manual analysis, or return an empirical inflated envelope whose failures remain measurable. Neither an observed maximum nor a training envelope certifies all operands or device states. Released aggregate rows cannot reconstruct repeat-level extremes, and isolated rewritten blocks do not validate whole-program energy composition. The [artifact's measurement scope](https://zenodo.org/records/11066623) must bound every conclusion.

## Strongest novelty objection and decision gate

The strongest objection is straightforward: this combines established code-deduplication hygiene, selective regression and safety margins on one public benchmark whose authors already discuss training diversity. A worsening deliberately adversarial split is unsurprising, and a detector that merely rejects long or rare-instruction blocks may offer no usable insight.

Retain only if a small preregistered pilot identifies meaningful residual overlap, reproduces baseline behavior adequately, and reveals a nontrivial matched-inflation reliability difference or a transferable support rule that beats global margins at useful retention. Control for block length and feature rarity. A null result can be a useful replication, but its publication value remains uncertain. Park the method-development claim if existing margins dominate, rejection is near-total, or no realistic deployment distribution justifies the split. Broader claims need another measured platform/corpus or independently measured programs, neither supplied by the current artifact.
